      what students take from it

      As the scholarship gets rarer, the model looks better and does worse. Pooled over 25 draws, accuracy climbs from 96.3% to 98.1% while the share of real winners it finds falls from 95.8% to 66.9%.

      A model can score well by ignoring the people it is meant to find. The lab asks students to predict that before they run it.

      Walking: 118 minutes and 9.3 km on the sidewalk. Two buses: 40 minutes, 750 m on foot, and 80% less modeled exposure.

      Exposure is the router’s modeled street-crime exposure along the route, scored by hour from 85,634 LAPD incidents, with the walk set to 100. A minute waiting at a stop is charged like 80 m of walking there. A model output, not a safety guarantee.
