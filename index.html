About

I’m a senior (in high school) at the Los Angeles Center for Enriched Studies. I build software for problems I care about, for people who need it, and I want to continue through college and my professional life.

My research is centred on machine learning, specifically natural language processing: telling which language model wrote a piece of text, and checking whether the tests we rank them with actually hold up. I’ve also interned across machine learning, software engineering and venture capital.

Off the laptop, I fence épée for Team USA, and I like eating, puzzles, the outdoors and napping.
