P.03

Safe Routes to School — Risk-Aware Routing (LA)

Walking and transit directions for LA students that route around the streets where kids actually get hurt. Scores 431,599 Los Angeles blocks for violent-crime risk from 85,634 filtered LAPD incidents — weighting recency, severity, and juvenile victims — then runs A* entirely in the browser to trade a few extra minutes for materially lower modeled exposure, the street-crime risk of the blocks a student walks. Riding the bus lowers it further, because minutes on board are minutes off the street. No backend, no API key.

3rd place · YCS Code for Transportation · 431,599 scored blocks

P.04

Ledger — License & Stocking Compliance for Small Stores

A corner store answers to eighteen programs across three levels of government. Enter them once and Ledger tracks every renewal date on a single calendar. Photograph a wholesale order record and a two-pass vision pipeline reads the line items, sorts them into dairy, grains, protein and produce, and scores the shelf against the SNAP stocking rule — seven varieties and twenty-one units in every category, with a perishable in three of the four — returning a pass/fail scorecard and a ranked fix list. Built to undercount: anything read below 0.75 confidence is held back and listed, not counted. English and Spanish throughout.

1st place · VISION HACK: South LA · 18 programs · EN/ES
Next.jsTypeScriptVision LLM
